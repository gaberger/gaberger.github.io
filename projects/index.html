Open source

Projects

Some of the things I’ve built and put in the open, mostly tools for working with AI agents, APIs and networks. Everything else is on GitHub.

Current

  • hexa

    A scaffolding system that grades the architecture of what it builds. An AI agent writes the code, and two gates decide whether it counts: a command that must exit 0 and an architecture grade that must hold.

    Rust · MIT · Alpha

    SiteGitHub

    Read: The Rules Nobody Wrote Down

  • Wardian

    Keeps the small apps you make, often with AI, on a computer you control and serves them to your browser. Each app runs sealed, with no view of your files or your other apps unless you allow it.

    Rust · MIT

    SiteGitHub

  • Earshot

    Private meeting notes that never leave your Mac. Records a meeting, then produces a transcript with speaker labels and a summary, all on-device with no account or cloud.

    Swift · MIT

    SiteGitHub

  • Forward MCP

    An MCP server that gives AI agents tools for working with the Forward Networks platform and its network data.

    Go · MIT

  • api-ai-readiness

    Grades an OpenAPI spec on how well it serves an AI agent, then generates a scorecard, a behavioral test suite and an MCP server.

    TypeScript · MIT

    Read: Delivering APIs for AI

  • Touchstone

    A knowledge base an agent compiles from your source material. Every claim cites where it came from, and the claims a person has checked are cryptographically signed.

    Rust · MIT

  • Deskman

    Tell your Mac where to put its windows, in plain words. Apple's on-device model understands the request, Deskman moves the windows, and nothing leaves your Mac.

    Swift · MIT

    SiteGitHub

  • Lens

    A live map of a Dygma Defy keyboard that floats over whatever you're working in on a Mac, and follows the active layer.

    Swift · GPL-3.0

Earlier

  • hostscan

    Turns an nmap scan into a hosts file, an Ansible inventory or a MongoDB inventory database.

    Python · Apache-2.0

  • nparser

    An example network configuration parser built with Instaparse.

    Clojure · Apache-2.0
